# Agent guides

Self-contained guidance for [mathematical writing and revision](math-papers.md),
[Emacs programming](emacs-programming-agent-guide.md),
and [Lean proofs](lean-proofs.md).

## Use

Copy the guide you need into your project and tell your agent to read it, or
refer to a local checkout from your personal agent instructions.  Keep this README with copies of the mathematics
guide so that its attribution travels with it.

The mathematics guide draws in part on [Mathematics Paper Skills](https://github.com/amelieloher/math-paper-skills) by Scott Armstrong and Amélie Loher, copyright 2026, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).  The current guide retains these adapted checks:

- Read from the reader's available text and identify explanations supplied from your own knowledge.
- Check explanatory prose against the proof, especially preservation versus improvement.
- Account for every old conclusion and needed estimate when restructuring.
- Test a broad rewrite on one representative passage with a difficult transition.
- Bind reviews to specific revisions and recheck affected findings after changes.
- Investigate a Lean mismatch rather than treating it as authority to change the paper.

These ideas have been condensed and combined with other preferences.
