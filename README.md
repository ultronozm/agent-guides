# Agent guides

Self-contained guidance for [mathematical writing and revision](math-papers.md)
and [Emacs programming and patch preparation](emacs-programming-agent-guide.md).
The guides contain the working advice and examples; the historical catalogues
are private background and are not part of the material intended for sharing.

## Attribution

The mathematics guide draws in part on [Mathematics Paper Skills](https://github.com/amelieloher/math-paper-skills) by Scott Armstrong and Amélie Loher, copyright 2026, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).  Adapted ideas include testing comprehension from the reader's text, checking explanatory prose against the proof, accounting for every conclusion during restructuring, trying a representative passage before a broad rewrite, and tying reviews to specific revisions.  These have been condensed and combined with independent writing preferences and examples; verification requirements have been adjusted to the scope of the edit.
